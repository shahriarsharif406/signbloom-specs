**Problem.** Sign language practice gives no feedback.
**Idea.** Copy a sign → it blooms into alpona → finish and draw your own.
**For.** Hearing family, teachers and classmates of Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in the Bangla-speaking world.

**Scope (finished, not sprawling).** 7 static one-handed signs · learn loop · reward painting · Bangla/English · accessible preset · demo mode for recording.

**Out of scope.** Motion signs, two-handed signs, full alphabet (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`).

**Biggest risk.** The starter signs are unreviewed. Mitigation: `tools/derive_curls.py` builds targets from the open BdSL47 data; educator review is the next step.
