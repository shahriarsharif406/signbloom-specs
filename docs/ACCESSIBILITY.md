# Accessibility

A sign-language learning tool has to be usable by the people it is meant to serve. What SignBloom does today, and what it does not yet.

## Built in

- **Accessible preset** (Inspector → Controller → Preset): hold time 1.6 s instead of 1.0 s, wider match tolerance, heavier smoothing against tremor, slower bloom, and star pacing scaled ×2 so slower learners are not penalised.
- **Forgiving hold:** a brief slip drains progress at 1.5× rather than resetting it.
- **Language-free feedback:** finger gauges (tall bar = extended finger) and a colour change on match mean the core loop does not depend on reading.
- **Bangla and English** for every string, coaching line and finger name.
- **Tap-to-start and Skip:** nobody is blocked waiting for hand detection or stuck on a sign.
- **No time pressure:** nothing fails or expires; stars reward speed but every learned sign earns at least one.
- **Left or right hand:** a single Inspector toggle.
- **Quiet by default:** audio cues are optional, short and soft.

## Not yet (honest list)

- Screen-reader/voice narration of the coaching line.
- A high-contrast colour theme (the dark theme is high-contrast but there is no alternative).
- One-handed operation of the menu buttons is possible but untested with real users.
- Educator review of the sign set and user testing with Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners — **the most important next step**.

## Design principle

Nothing is taught that has not been reviewed by the community it comes from. SignBloom is a practice and motivation tool built to be corrected by Deaf educators, not a replacement for them.
